Help

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous trading experience?

No. Folder 01 starts from the very beginning — what Forex is, how currency pairs are quoted, and what pips, lots, spreads and leverage mean. Each folder builds on the previous one, so working through them in order is recommended.

Will this academy tell me what to buy or sell?

No. ICEBACK FOREX ACADEMY is an education platform. We teach concepts, patterns, risk frameworks and analysis methods. We do not provide trade signals, personalised investment advice, or portfolio management.

Is any of the chart data live?

No. Every chart, candle example and account balance on this platform is simulated and clearly labelled as educational. We do not stream live market data or connect to brokerage accounts.

What do the certificates represent?

Certificates confirm that you completed the lessons and quizzes of an educational module. They are not regulated financial qualifications and carry no professional licence or accreditation.

How does mentorship enrolment work?

Choose a package on the Mentorship page and start checkout with our payment provider. Your enrolment is activated only after the provider sends a verified payment confirmation to our server. If a payment is not verified, no access is granted.

How do I reach support?

Call or WhatsApp +263 78 837 7887 or +263 78 884 8481, or use the contact form. Support hours are Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00 and Sat 09:00–13:00 (CAT, UTC+2).
